            Want a more natural voice?

            iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → pick a UK voice marked Enhanced or Premium (for example Serena or Daniel) and download it. Then come back here and choose it.

            Android: Settings → search “Text-to-speech” → Preferred engine: Speech Services by Google → Language: English (United Kingdom) → install the voice data, and pick a voice you like. Then choose it here.
