# Delivery round sat nav

Training sat nav for delivery rounds. The main page is the Annington round. Open the live page on a phone and tap Start sat nav.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## Ashurst round (test)

`/ashurst/` is a second sat nav built from the sorting frame (shelves 1 to 14, left to right). Pins marked "best guess" were placed on the nearest likely building and need checking on the ground.

## How it works

- **Start screen:** pick *Learn the round* (every house, frame order) or *Parcels today* (tick the houses you have parcels for and it routes to just those, still in frame order).
- **Find a house:** search bar on the map. Name, number, road or "shelf 9".
- **Rounds:** tap the round name to switch rounds.
- **Sat nav:** moves on by itself when you stop at a house and drive or walk off.
